How the comparison works

The simulator steps the projectile forward 0.02 seconds at a time, applying gravity and air drag that grows with the square of speed. It is the reference answer.

The neural network never sees those equations. It was trained on simulated launches and predicts the whole flight in one step: 100 points along the path, the impact point and the flight time. Each dot on the plot is one of those 100 points, drawn exactly as predicted.

The sliders stop at the limits the network was trained on, so every launch here is one it should handle. Both calculations run in your browser. Switch to the 3D view to see the heading as well, and drag to look from any angle.
